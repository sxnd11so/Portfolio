Our Story

Welcome to iiiso's library, where the love of music meets the art of collecting. We're more than just a vinyl store – we're a community of music enthusiasts dedicated to preserving the authentic sound and tactile experience that only vinyl can provide.

Founded by a passionate collector and self-taught developer, our journey began with a simple belief: music deserves to be experienced in its purest form. Every groove, every crackle, every note tells a story that digital formats simply can't capture.

Today, we curate a carefully selected collection of albums spanning genres from classic hip-hop to timeless rock, contemporary R&B to legendary jazz. Each record in our collection is chosen for its musical significance and audio quality, ensuring that every purchase is an investment in your musical journey.
